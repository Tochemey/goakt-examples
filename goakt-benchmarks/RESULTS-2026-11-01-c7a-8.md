## Run notes

- **v4.6.7 is the same code as `f7ee4c6`.** The `v4.6.7` tag points at commit `9b0ca26`, which only adds to the changelog (`changelogs/v4.6.7.md` and `changelogs/unreleased.md`) on top of `f7ee4c6`. Every Go file and GoAkt's `go.mod` are identical, so these figures are v4.6.7's.
- **GoAkt was rerun on its own.** The 19:23 run measured GoAkt at commit `4e82bfc`, and a 20:48 rerun measured it at `9978ee1`. GoAkt was then rerun alone at `f7ee4c6`, and its figures here replace both.
  - **Possibly a different physical host.** The instance was stopped, resized and started several times between these runs, so each may have run on a different physical machine. The instance type, OS, Go version and benchmark code were the same.
  - **One dependency changed with the commit.** `f7ee4c6` requires `github.com/cenkalti/backoff/v7` v7.0.3 instead of v7.0.1. Only GoAkt uses that library.
  - **The other frameworks are unaffected by the GoAkt commit.** Each framework runs in its own test binary.
  - **The replaced figures are kept.** The earlier GoAkt output is in `results/2026-11-01-c7a-8/goakt-4e82bfc.txt.old` and `goakt-9978ee1.txt.old`.
- **Machine:** before each run, unattended upgrades, the apt timers and snapd were stopped, and nothing else ran on the instance.
- **Steal time:** `vmstat` sampled every 5 seconds and reported 0% steal time in all 353 samples of the full run and all 63 samples of the GoAkt rerun.
- **Completeness:** every round of every framework completed, with no errors, panics or timeouts. Ergo and Hollywood have 140 result lines each. GoAkt and Proto.Actor have 180 each, because they also run the four grain benchmarks.
- **Running time:**

  | Framework | Time |
  |---|---|
  | GoAkt | 308 s |
  | Ergo | 212 s |
  | Proto.Actor | 912 s |
  | Hollywood | 322 s |

- **Reading the tables:**
  - Each cell is the median of 10 runs. The best value in each row is in bold, and the factor in parentheses is relative to GoAkt.
  - The `-8` suffix is `GOMAXPROCS`.
  - `Skynet`, `MemoryPerActor` and `GrainMemoryPerGrain` ran once per round, so their medians are over 10 iterations.
- **Wide spreads.** These rows vary by about ±10% or more between rounds, according to benchstat:

  | Framework | Benchmark | Spread |
  |---|---|---|
  | GoAkt | `TellFromActor` | ±14% time, ±16% throughput |
  | Proto.Actor | `TellFromActor` | ±24% time, ±22% throughput |
  | Proto.Actor | `PingPongPipelined` | ±14% time, ±12% throughput |
  | Proto.Actor | `TellParallel` | ±10% time, ±11% throughput |
  | Proto.Actor | `GrainActivate` | ±10% time; bytes ±78%, allocations ±93% |
  | Ergo | `Skynet` | ±11% time, ±10% throughput |

  - **GoAkt's `TellFromActor` rounds fell into two groups.** Four took 44.4 to 48.0 ns and six took 54.1 to 55.0 ns. The median, 54.1 ns, is in the slower group. Hollywood's rounds stayed within ±2% of 45.6 ns.
  - **Bytes per operation** vary more where the values are only a few bytes: GoAkt's `TellParallel` (11 B, ±27%) and `TellFromActor` (29 B, ±14%), and Proto.Actor's `TellFromActor` (36 B, ±36%).

  None of this changes a ranking.
- **Near-ties that the bold in the tables does not show:**
  - `GrainActivate`: Proto.Actor's time is 3.6% lower than GoAkt's (16.34 µs against 16.94 µs, p=0.000). It is significant but within Proto.Actor's own ±10% spread.
  - `GrainActivate` bytes and allocations: benchstat finds no significant difference (p=0.136 and p=0.111), although the table bolds GoAkt. Proto.Actor's median of 921 allocations varies by ±93% between rounds.

## What the numbers show

**GoAkt leads**
- **Ping-pong shapes:**

  | Benchmark | GoAkt | Next best |
  |---|---|---|
  | `PingPongParallel` (one pair per core) | 47.71M msgs/s | Hollywood, 15.41M (3.1×) |
  | `PingPongPipelined` (100 in flight per pair) | 142.88M msgs/s | Proto.Actor, 96.48M |
  | `PingPong` | 7.86M msgs/s | Hollywood, 4.24M |
  | `ThreadRing` | 7.92M msgs/s | Hollywood, 3.81M |

  GoAkt allocates nothing per message in any of these.
- **Fan-out and many-to-many:** `ForkJoinThroughput` 26.97M msgs/s against Proto.Actor's 10.68M. `Big` 35.54M against Hollywood's 15.52M.
- **Request/response:** `Ask` 1.30M msgs/s against Hollywood's 666K. `AskParallel` 2.11M against Ergo's 843K and Proto.Actor's 821K. GoAkt makes 2 allocations per request, the other frameworks 4 to 12.
- **`TellParallel`:** 18.23M msgs/s against Hollywood's 16.55M (54.9 ns against 60.4 ns per message, p=0.000), then Proto.Actor's 10.48M and Ergo's 4.68M.
- **Grains:**
  - `GrainAsk` takes 761 ns in GoAkt with no allocation, against 5.32 µs and 21 allocations in Proto.Actor.
  - `GrainAskParallel`: GoAkt moves 4.34M msgs/s against Proto.Actor's 2.02M.
  - An idle grain keeps 398 B of live heap in GoAkt and 1,624 B in Proto.Actor.

**GoAkt trails**
- **`TellFromActor`:** Hollywood moves 21.94M msgs/s (45.6 ns), 1.19× GoAkt's 18.48M (54.1 ns). Proto.Actor moves 13.46M and Ergo 4.76M.
- **`Tell`, narrowly:** Hollywood moves 22.46M msgs/s (44.5 ns), 1.07× GoAkt's 21.07M (47.5 ns), p=0.001. Proto.Actor moves 18.44M and Ergo 5.66M.
- **Actor lifecycle:**
  - `Skynet`: Proto.Actor creates and stops 1.30M actors/s, 2.86× GoAkt's 456K. Ergo manages 572K and Hollywood 88K.
  - `SpawnStop`: Proto.Actor runs at 233K actors/s, 1.17× GoAkt's 199K. GoAkt is ahead of Ergo (165K) and Hollywood (160K).
- **Memory per idle actor:** 616 B for Proto.Actor, 1,065 B for GoAkt, 2,879 B for Ergo and 27,880 B for Hollywood.

**Tied**
- **`GrainActivate`:** 16.94 µs for GoAkt and 16.34 µs for Proto.Actor (see above).
