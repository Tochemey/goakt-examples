## Run notes

- **v4.6.7 is the same code as `f7ee4c6`.** The `v4.6.7` tag points at commit `9b0ca26`, which only adds to the changelog (`changelogs/v4.6.7.md` and `changelogs/unreleased.md`) on top of `f7ee4c6`. Every Go file and GoAkt's `go.mod` are identical, so these figures are v4.6.7's.
- **GoAkt was rerun on its own.** The 18:42 run measured GoAkt at commit `4e82bfc`. GoAkt was then rerun alone at `f7ee4c6`, and its figures here replace the earlier ones.
  - **Possibly a different physical host.** The instance was stopped, resized and started several times between the two runs, so it may have run on a different physical machine. The instance type, OS, Go version and benchmark code were the same.
  - **One dependency changed with the commit.** `f7ee4c6` requires `github.com/cenkalti/backoff/v7` v7.0.3 instead of v7.0.1. Only GoAkt uses that library.
  - **The other frameworks are unaffected by the GoAkt commit.** Each framework runs in its own test binary.
  - **The replaced figures are kept.** The `4e82bfc` GoAkt output is in `results/2026-11-01-c7a-64/goakt-4e82bfc.txt.old`.
- **Machine:** before each run, unattended upgrades, the apt timers and snapd were stopped, and nothing else ran on the instance.
- **Steal time:** `vmstat` sampled every 5 seconds and reported 0% steal time in all 382 samples of the full run and all 72 samples of the GoAkt rerun.
- **Completeness:** every round of every framework completed, with no errors, panics or timeouts. Ergo and Hollywood have 140 result lines each. GoAkt and Proto.Actor have 180 each, because they also run the four grain benchmarks.
- **Running time:**

  | Framework | Time |

  | GoAkt | 354 s |
  | Ergo | 211 s |
  | Proto.Actor | 912 s |
  | Hollywood | 449 s |

- **Reading the tables:**

  - The `-64` suffix is `GOMAXPROCS`.
  - `Skynet`, `MemoryPerActor` and `GrainMemoryPerGrain` ran once per round, so their medians are over 10 iterations.
- **Wide spreads.** These rows vary by about ±10% or more between rounds, according to benchstat:

  | Framework | Benchmark | Spread |

  | GoAkt | `TellFromActor` | ±24% time, ±32% throughput |

  | Hollywood | `TellParallel` | ±10% time, ±11% throughput |

  - **GoAkt's `TellFromActor` rounds:** in run order, they took 67.5, 93.9, 73.3, 96.9, 99.6, 104.0, 101.8, 95.6, 102.3 and 96.4 ns. Two of the first three rounds were about 30% faster than the rest. The median, 96.6 ns, sits among the slower rounds. Even GoAkt's fastest round, 67.5 ns, was slower than Hollywood's median, 55.9 ns, so the ranking holds.
  - **Proto.Actor's `Skynet`:** its slowest round (778 ms) is still 3.3× faster than GoAkt's fastest (2,530 ms).

  None of this changes a ranking.
- **Statistical tie:** benchstat finds no significant difference between GoAkt and Proto.Actor on `GrainActivate` (p=0.105), although the table bolds Proto.Actor's median.

## What the numbers show

**GoAkt leads**
- **Ping-pong shapes, by the widest margins of the run:**

  | Benchmark | GoAkt | Next best |
  |---|---|---|
  | `PingPongParallel` (one pair per core) | 180.10M msgs/s | Proto.Actor, 16.80M (10.7×) |
  | `PingPongPipelined` (100 in flight per pair) | 602.00M msgs/s | Proto.Actor, 166.17M (3.6×) |
  | `PingPong` | 7.88M msgs/s | Hollywood, 3.70M |
  | `ThreadRing` | 7.85M msgs/s | Hollywood, 3.00M |

  GoAkt allocates nothing per message in any of these.
- **Fan-out and many-to-many:** `ForkJoinThroughput` 8.02M msgs/s against Proto.Actor's 3.76M. `Big` 22.25M against Proto.Actor's 16.43M.
- **Request/response:** `Ask` 960K msgs/s against Hollywood's 515K. `AskParallel` 1.32M against Ergo's 764K. GoAkt makes 2 allocations per request, the other frameworks 4 to 12.
- **`TellParallel`, by a narrow margin:** 5.89M msgs/s against Proto.Actor's 5.58M (5.7% faster, p=0.001), then Hollywood's 4.20M and Ergo's 2.15M.
- **Grains:**
  - `GrainAsk` takes 1.04 µs in GoAkt with no allocation, against 5.90 µs and 21 allocations in Proto.Actor.
  - An idle grain keeps 398 B of live heap in GoAkt and 1,620 B in Proto.Actor.

**GoAkt trails**
- **Sends with no reply:**
  - `TellFromActor`: Hollywood moves 17.91M msgs/s (55.9 ns), 1.73× GoAkt's 10.35M (96.6 ns). Proto.Actor moves 7.33M and Ergo 2.29M.
  - `Tell`: Hollywood is fastest at 17.38M msgs/s (57.6 ns), 1.21× GoAkt's 14.39M (69.5 ns). Proto.Actor follows at 11.95M and Ergo at 2.45M.
- **Actor lifecycle:**
  - `Skynet`: Proto.Actor creates and stops 2.22M actors/s, 5.10× GoAkt's 436K. Ergo manages 893K and Hollywood 48K.
  - `SpawnStop`: Proto.Actor runs at 186K actors/s, 1.32× GoAkt's 141K. GoAkt is ahead of Hollywood (138K, 2.4% slower than GoAkt, p=0.011) and Ergo (130K).
- **`GrainAskParallel`:** with 64 callers each on its own grain, Proto.Actor moves 2.63M msgs/s, 1.46× GoAkt's 1.81M.
- **Memory per idle actor:** 616 B for Proto.Actor, 1,065 B for GoAkt, 2,879 B for Ergo and 27,880 B for Hollywood.

**Tied**
- **`GrainActivate`:** 18.50 µs for GoAkt and 17.89 µs for Proto.Actor (p=0.105). Proto.Actor allocates less per activation: 3.8 KiB and 66 allocations, against GoAkt's 8.7 KiB and 123.
