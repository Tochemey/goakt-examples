## Run notes

- **v4.6.7 is the same code as `f7ee4c6`.** The `v4.6.7` tag points at commit `9b0ca26`, which only adds to the changelog (`changelogs/v4.6.7.md` and `changelogs/unreleased.md`) on top of `f7ee4c6`. Every Go file and GoAkt's `go.mod` are identical, so these figures are v4.6.7's.
- **GoAkt was rerun on its own.** The 21:15 run measured GoAkt at commit `9978ee1`. GoAkt was then rerun alone at `f7ee4c6`, and its figures here replace the earlier ones.
  - **Same machine.** The instance was not stopped between the two runs, so both ran on the same physical host.
  - **One dependency changed with the commit.** `f7ee4c6` requires `github.com/cenkalti/backoff/v7` v7.0.3 instead of v7.0.1. Only GoAkt uses that library.
  - **The other frameworks are unaffected by the GoAkt commit.** Each framework runs in its own test binary.
  - **The replaced figures are kept.** The `9978ee1` GoAkt output is in `results/2026-11-01-c7a-32/goakt-9978ee1.txt.old`.
- **Machine:** before the run, unattended upgrades, the apt timers and snapd were stopped, and nothing else ran on the instance.
- **Steal time:** `vmstat` sampled every 5 seconds and reported 0% steal time in all 375 samples of the full run and all 73 samples of the GoAkt rerun.
- **Completeness:** every round of every framework completed, with no errors, panics or timeouts. Ergo and Hollywood have 140 result lines each. GoAkt and Proto.Actor have 180 each, because they also run the four grain benchmarks.
- **Running time:**

  | Framework | Time |
  |---|---|
  | GoAkt | 358 s |
  | Ergo | 206 s |
  | Proto.Actor | 894 s |
  | Hollywood | 412 s |

- **Reading the tables:**
  - Each cell is the median of 10 runs. The best value in each row is in bold, and the factor in parentheses is relative to GoAkt.
  - The `-32` suffix is `GOMAXPROCS`.
  - `Skynet`, `MemoryPerActor` and `GrainMemoryPerGrain` ran once per round, so their medians are over 10 iterations.
- **Spreads.** According to benchstat, these rows vary by about ±10% or more between rounds:

  | Ergo | `TellParallel` | ±12% time, ±14% throughput |

  Bytes per operation vary more where the values are only a few bytes: GoAkt's `Tell` (20.5 B, ±27%), `TellParallel` (2 B, ±50%) and `TellFromActor` (24 B, ±21%), and Proto.Actor's `Tell` (26.5 B, ±55%). None of this changes a ranking.

## What the numbers show

**GoAkt leads**
- **Ping-pong shapes:**

  | Benchmark | GoAkt | Next best |
  |---|---|---|
  | `PingPongParallel` (one pair per core) | 94.21M msgs/s | Proto.Actor, 12.28M (7.7×) |
  | `PingPongPipelined` (100 in flight per pair) | 330.73M msgs/s | Proto.Actor, 162.59M (2.0×) |
  | `PingPong` | 7.88M msgs/s | Hollywood, 3.70M |
  | `ThreadRing` | 7.84M msgs/s | Hollywood, 3.02M |

  GoAkt allocates nothing per message in any of these.
- **Fan-out and many-to-many:** `ForkJoinThroughput` 9.13M msgs/s against Proto.Actor's 4.83M. `Big` 21.58M against Proto.Actor's 13.99M.
- **Request/response:** `Ask` 1.07M msgs/s against Hollywood's 545K. `AskParallel` 1.19M against Ergo's 625K. GoAkt makes 2 allocations per request, the other frameworks 4 to 13.
- **`TellParallel`:** 8.38M msgs/s against Proto.Actor's 5.67M and Hollywood's 5.41M, then Ergo's 1.89M.
- **Grains:**
  - `GrainAsk` takes 911 ns in GoAkt with no allocation, against 5.92 µs and 21 allocations in Proto.Actor.
  - An idle grain keeps 399 B of live heap in GoAkt and 1,624 B in Proto.Actor.

**GoAkt trails**
- **`TellFromActor`:** Hollywood moves 17.67M msgs/s (56.6 ns), 1.24× GoAkt's 14.27M (70.1 ns). Proto.Actor moves 7.81M and Ergo 2.54M.
- **`Tell`:** Hollywood moves 19.36M msgs/s (51.7 ns), 1.26× GoAkt's 15.41M (64.9 ns). GoAkt is ahead of Proto.Actor's 13.08M (76.5 ns) and Ergo's 2.77M.
- **Actor lifecycle:**
  - `Skynet`: Proto.Actor creates and stops 2.48M actors/s, 5.87× GoAkt's 422K. Ergo manages 1.08M and Hollywood 56K.
  - `SpawnStop`: Proto.Actor runs at 194K actors/s, 1.32× GoAkt's 147K. GoAkt is ahead of Ergo (140K) and Hollywood (139K).
- **`GrainAskParallel`:** with 32 callers each on its own grain, Proto.Actor moves 2.60M msgs/s, 1.15× GoAkt's 2.25M.
- **`GrainActivate`, narrowly:** Proto.Actor takes 17.37 µs against GoAkt's 17.79 µs, 2.4% faster (p=0.035). It also allocates less per activation: 3.8 KiB and 66 allocations, against GoAkt's 8.7 KiB and 123.
- **Memory per idle actor:** 616 B for Proto.Actor, 1,064 B for GoAkt, 2,879 B for Ergo and 27,880 B for Hollywood.
